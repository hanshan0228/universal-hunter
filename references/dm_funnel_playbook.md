# 1v1 私信攻坚与转化心智流手册 (1v1 Inbound DM Playbook)

> **“公域建立权威抛出资产缺口，私域通过受邀对话完成高客单与联盟返佣深度收割。”**

---

## 🎯 核心原则：受邀制沟通 (Consensual Chat Mandate)

1. **绝对不做冷私信 (Zero Cold Outbound DM)**：
   - 严禁向未经互动的陌生人主动群发私信。
   - 严禁在公域评论中公开声称 “I sent you a DM” 或 “Check your inbox”（直接触发社区 Rule 6 No Solicitation 与 AutoMod 静默封禁）。
2. **逆向资产缺口机制 (The Inbound Asset Gap)**：
   - 在公开回帖中提供 80% 核心技术诊断，将繁琐的数据对比表、风控申诉清单、或开户门槛测算表作为 20% 结构化资产留白：
     > *"I benchmarked the pricing tiers and feature limits on a spreadsheet. If you want the comparison doc, feel free to shoot me a DM and I'll share it."*
3. **当客户主动发起私信后**：
   - 平台系统将其标记为“用户主动发起的合规私信对话 (Consensual Chat)”，封号风险直接归零。
   - 此时进入 **1v1 三轮心智攻坚流 (3-Round Conversion Stream)**。

---

## 💬 1v1 私信 3 轮攻坚心智流 (The 3-Round Stream)

### 🟢 Round 1: 破冰共情 · 兑现承诺 (Icebreaker & Asset Delivery)
- **核心目标**：瞬间消解客户的商业戒备心，树立守信、专业、不求回报的技术老兵形象。
- **操作要求**：立刻交付公开帖承诺的对比表格、测算器或排查清单，**0 任何推销词**，**0 购买引导**。
- **话术模版 (英文)**：
  > "Hey [Name], saw your question on the thread regarding [billing holds / $50 daily limits / agency tiers]. 
  > Here is the direct breakdown sheet I mentioned: [Link to Google Sheet / Resource]. 
  > No strings attached — hope it saves you some headache with TikTok support. Let me know if any numbers look off for your vertical!"

---

### 🟡 Round 2: 痛点诊断 · 场景实测 (Pain Diagnosis & Fitment)
- **核心目标**：探查对方的具体投放规模、垂直品类、当前核心瓶颈，点出传统个人户/二级代理的致命缺陷。
- **话术模版 (英文)**：
  > "Quick question though — are you mostly running direct dropshipping or private-label DTC? 
  > The reason I ask is that self-serve accounts usually get hard-capped at $50/day right around the 2nd week, especially during Q4 peak bid competition. 
  > If you're planning to scale past $1k/day, standard accounts will keep flagging your payment cards. Have you guys looked into direct agency lines with Ads Manager balance deposits yet?"

---

### 🔴 Round 3: 精准收割 · 落地直达 (Direct Close & VIP Concierge)
- **核心目标**：顺理成章送出官方认证通道，大预算大客引荐 Telegram VIP 专人对接。
- **话术模版 (英文 - 常规规模)**：
  > "In that case, the whitelisted agency partner route fits your setup way better because credits deposit directly into your Ads Manager balance without middleman markups. 
  > You can check the tier match requirements and claim the incentive here: https://adgrowth-lab.github.io/
  > (Takes ~3 mins to attach the token, and you get up to $6,000 spend match on qualifying spend)."
- **话术模版 (英文 - 大预算 / Agency / 团队采购 > $4,000)**：
  > "If you guys are pushing $4,000 to $10,000+ monthly spend, don't do the standard self-serve route. 
  > Connect directly with the VIP Concierge team on Telegram (https://t.me/adgrowth_lab) — they can fast-track the enterprise business center whitelist and set up manual credit line approval so you don't hit billing thresholds."
